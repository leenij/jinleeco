Patient experience

Phil Kindbody Mobile

Designed Kindbody's patient-facing mobile app for appointment booking, care management, and to-do tracking. The app needed to feel warm and approachable in a clinical domain that can be stressful for patients.

The design balanced clear task management with an encouraging, supportive tone — helping patients stay on top of their care plan without feeling overwhelmed.
