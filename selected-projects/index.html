About

I'm a design leader in New York. I've spent the last several years building design practices inside healthcare and education companies — hiring teams, setting direction, and working closely with product and engineering to ship things that actually work for the people using them.

I care most about the work between the pixels: understanding the problem clearly, building the right team culture, and making sure design decisions hold up when they hit real users and real constraints. The best design work I've done has been in messy, high-stakes domains where getting it right matters.
